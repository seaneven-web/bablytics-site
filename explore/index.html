The Boardroom, in public

7 sessions · sixteen seats · real market data

Bablytics runs a sixteen-seat AI investment board every weekday: it builds a slate from real market data, has every seat grade it from its own philosophy, lets a Skeptic attack the result, and files the whole argument. This is the archive of what it actually produced — the books, the grades, the cross-examinations and the corrections, exactly as written on the day.

What is not here.

Positions, entries and results belonging to the person running this desk are removed before anything is published — by an allowlist that publishes only known board fields, a second pass that drops any single sentence mentioning them, and a scanner that fails the build if any of it survives anyway. You are reading what the board thought, not what anyone holds.
